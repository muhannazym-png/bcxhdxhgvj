01 — A LETTER FOR YOU
❀

Dear Mom,

Sometimes I wonder how to explain just how much you mean to me. How do I put years of love, care, warmth, and countless little moments into a few paragraphs?

You have been there through so many stages of my life. From the days when I was little and needed your hand to hold, to the days when I am growing up and slowly learning to find my own way.

No matter how much I grow, a part of me will always be that little child who feels safe knowing that Mom is near.

Thank you for the things you do without being asked, for the worries you carry quietly, for the times you put my happiness before your own, and for all the love you give in ways I may not always notice right away.

I know I may not say it every day, but I notice more than you think. And I am grateful for more than I can say.

I hope life gives you back all the kindness you have given to me. I hope you have peaceful mornings, reasons to smile, dreams that come true, and days when you feel as loved as you have always made me feel.

No matter where life takes me, a piece of my heart will always belong to you.

I love you endlessly,
Your child, always. ♡
